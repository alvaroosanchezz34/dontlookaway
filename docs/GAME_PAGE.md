## Icon direction

Close-up of a door slightly open onto a dark corridor. Through the gap: an extremely
tall, thin humanoid figure. A single white light (a flashlight beam from the viewer)
catches part of its silhouette — the long arm, the small head pushed forward. No text.

How to capture it in Studio:
1. Start a round (Admin panel → *Start round*, or ready up alone).
2. Admin panel → *Sighting* or *Spawn Observer* to place The Observer in the
   upper corridor; stand in a doorway of an office facing out, door half open.
3. Turn your flashlight on and point it so the beam grazes the figure.
4. Hide the HUD (Settings / screenshot mode in Studio), use FOV 60, take a 1:1 crop.

## Thumbnails

1. **The impossible door** — first person, a long corridor; a door that should not
   be there on the left wall (force `EXTRA_DOOR` or `WRONG_ROOM` from the Admin panel).
2. **Four players, one figure** — four players at one end of the main corridor,
   flashlights converging on The Observer standing still at the far end.
3. **A friend who isn't** — a player's flashlight on a "teammate" in a doorway whose
   name tag is glitching (Admin panel → *Mimic*).
4. **Before / after** — split frame: the same office normal on the left, altered on
   the right (lowered ceiling, coloured light, floating furniture: force
   `CEILING_LOW`, `LIGHTS_CHANGE`, `FLOATING`).

Keep the images uncluttered: one idea per thumbnail, no large text overlays.
